# Fantastic person card — designer handoff

This card is what players photograph. Every card is the same artwork. Only the footer code and QR change per person. The app locks onto the corner marks and reads the QR. Name and company are handwritten.

## Finished size

148 mm wide × 100 mm tall, landscape.

Bleed 3 mm on the orange header only. Keep the corner marks and the QR inside the trim.

## Stock and finish

300–350 gsm uncoated matte white. No gloss, laminate, or spot UV on the write area. Glare fights the phone flash.

## Color

| Use | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Header band | `#fc4d0f` |
| Wordmark on the header | white |
| Rules, labels, corner marks, code | `#193abe` or black |
| Write field | white paper, not printed orange |

- Header, about 22 mm: Fanta wordmark and a short lockup such as “Fantastic People”.
- White field: printed labels `NAME` and `COMPANY`. Each writing box is about 16 mm tall. Rules are light gray, not black, so they do not fight the handwriting.
- One line of micro type: `BLOCK CAPITALS · black marker`.
- Footer, about 18 mm: a white box with a black monospace code (`FP-00482`) and a QR about 18 mm. Quiet zone around the QR. These two are the only variable data.

## Marks that must be identical on every card

Four black L-marks, 6 mm, inset about 4 mm from the trim, outside the writing boxes. The in-app guide copies this frame. Do not decorate them, move them, or change their weight.

## QR and code

- Human code: `FP-` plus five digits, black, monospace, high contrast on white.
- QR encodes `FANTA:FP-00482` (the same code, with the `FANTA:` prefix).
- The printer supplies variable data. Designers deliver one template.

## Do not

- Print the write field in orange.
- Run handwriting over the QR or the code.
- Put patterns, photos, or texture in the write boxes.
- Use a glossy finish.
- Make illustrations that differ per card.

## Registration desk

One pre-printed unique card per person, plus black markers. Cards are not reused.
